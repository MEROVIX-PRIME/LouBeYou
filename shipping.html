Shipping & Delivery

Everything you need to know about getting your Lou

Where we deliver

At the moment, delivery is available only within Vietnam. We are working on expanding to other countries — stay tuned!

Shipping cost

Delivery across Vietnam is completely free for all orders. No minimum order amount, no hidden fees.

How to order

Choose the items you like, add them to your cart, and fill in your details — name, phone number, email, city, and delivery address.

After you submit your order, our manager will contact you to confirm the details and arrange delivery. Payment is made upon delivery.

Delivery time

Delivery usually takes 3–7 business days depending on your location within Vietnam.
