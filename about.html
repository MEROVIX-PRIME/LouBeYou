A little world called Lou

A story that began with one little thought

My name is Anastasia, and I am the author and creator of Lou.

Lou was born from a simple desire — to create something kind.

I have always wanted there to be a little more warmth, love, and kindness in the world. So that amidst the endless rush, tasks, and thoughts, there would still be room for something simple and real.

And so Lou appeared.

Small, a little funny, sometimes thoughtful, but always real.
He doesn't teach you how to live, and he doesn't try to give you the right answers.
He simply reminds you of something we so often forget.

The most important things are not always the biggest ones.

Sometimes it is a cup of your favorite coffee in the morning.

A song you want to listen to again and again.

One more page of a book before bed.

A random smile.

A walk with no destination.

A moment when, suddenly, everything feels calm.

We often walk past these moments because we are too busy thinking about tomorrow. But it is precisely these little moments that our lives are made of.

Lou was created to remind you of that.

So that when you look at him, you smile.
So that some little phrase makes you stop for a second.
So that you remember that life is not only about big events.
It is also about the little moments we feel.

And perhaps, this is where our happiness is hiding.

Why Lou?

Because I wanted to create a character with nothing unnecessary.

Lou is not perfect. He is simply Lou.

I decided to make his image soft and pleasant — something you would want to touch, even if you only see him on a T-shirt print, on a mug, or on another item.

I wanted him to create a feeling of warmth and coziness. So that you would want to keep him close, carry him with you, and smile when you look at him.

And his sneakers with untied shoelaces became his main feature.

There is something sweetly messy about them — a little careless, a little funny, but very real. They complement his character and make Lou simply Lou.

He is not lazy.
He is just a little bit quirky.

He may not be in a hurry to go anywhere, get lost in his own thoughts,
forget to tie his shoelaces, or simply stop in the middle of the day
and enjoy the moment.

And maybe that is exactly why I like him so much.
Because Lou does not try to be perfect.
He simply stays true to himself.

Be You.

Lou has one main thought:

Be You.

You don't have to be someone else.
You don't always have to become better, faster, or more successful.

Sometimes it is enough to simply be yourself, notice what makes you happy, and allow yourself to enjoy the little things.

Lou is a little reminder to slow down, feel more, and be yourself.

With love,

Anastasia

Author and creator of Lou.
